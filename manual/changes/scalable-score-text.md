With `BitmapGameFont=no`, enlarged campaign and multiplayer score text and software menu labels use a scalable face. Score letters keep their colors, spacing, and typing animation. Enlarged match labels also fit their descenders inside the original line height. See [Scalable text](/using/scalable-text/).
